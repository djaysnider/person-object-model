# Legacy System with Documentation

This repository contains my resume.

Most resumes are Word documents.
Some are PDFs.
A few are websites.

Mine is currently YAML because after 30 years in technology, 
I trust source control more than recruiters.

Eventually, it will be a narrative accounting of my personality, technical and creative output, likes and dislikes in a job, and everything else that should be more relevant to a recruiter than the fact that I was GenTran certified back in 1998.

Would a human care?  Would they read it?  No.

Would a future AI recruiter find it effortless to consume and useful in identifying my fit for a job and company? No, but they should.

I envision the death of the outmoded, usually ignored resume/CV. I believe, in the future, technology will scan the entirety of my experience, skillset, and passions, and finally confirm what, subconsciously, I have known all along.  Darrin, based on everything you are, want to be, and love doing, your ideal position is not CMMC-compliant cloud operations.  It's either:
* Assistant Museum Curator/Marketing Director in Kaunas, Lithuania
* Overnight Freeform Disc Jockey in Weaver Bay or similar tourist trap beachfront town
* Product evangelist for currently undiscovered bizarre sport
* Head of R&D for a radical new form of Circus that incorporates both Barnum and Baudrillard

Unlike most enterprise systems of similar age,
I will be soon be fully documented, actively maintained,
cloud-compatible, and unlikely to be retired soon.
